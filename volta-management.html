05 / 06 · Case study

Volta Management

A website for Volta, a football development agency that helps players go from grassroots to professional level. This was a group project with a real client.

Role UX / UI Designer Type Client Project Year 2025 Tools Figma
Problem

Volta didn't have a website, so parents and players couldn't see how its player development works.

What I did

Spoke to a founder, the owners and players, then designed the Player Development and About Us pages as part of a team of three.

Result

Testers understood the site straight away, and the client was really happy with it.

Research

Talking to the people involved.

Volta had never had a website, so there was nothing to review. Instead, we spoke to the people behind it.

One of the founders gave us a presentation on how they develop players, the models they follow, and why they focus on long-term development over quick results. We also asked the owners and some players what they'd want to see on a website.

They follow set modelsVolta has a few clear development models, so these became the core of the Player Development page.
Long-term over short-termThe founder made it clear that Volta is about long-term development, not quick results.
Parents and players are the audienceThe site needed to show families what Volta stands for and how players develop there.
My role

My role.

I was part of a team of three. I was responsible for the Player Development page, the About Us page and the idea for the promo video.

Decision 01 / 03

Make the models easy to understand

What I found
Volta's development is based on clear models, but parents and players had nowhere to learn about them.
What I did
I put each model on its own card with a short summary and a “Read more” link, so families can get the overview quickly and read more about what interests them.
Decision 02 / 03

Tell a player's story

What I found
Volta cares most about a player's long-term journey.
What I did
We suggested a homepage video that follows a player from grassroots football to signing a professional contract.
Decision 03 / 03

Show how far Volta reaches

What I found
There was no way to see how many places Volta had placed players.
What I did
An interactive world map shows where Volta's players have gone, which says more than a block of text could.
Wireframes

From sketches to layouts.

We sketched out the main pages and then built more detailed wireframes before starting the visual design.

Testing

Testing.

What we foundPeople understood what the site was for straight away and found the buttons clear. The Player Development page was harder to find than we expected.
What we changedWe made the page easier to find in the navigation and moved some buttons to make it more visible.
Outcome

Client feedback.

Beng'yela at Volta said we really understood the brand, and especially liked the homepage video idea, the world map and the way we presented the player pathway.

Final design

Final pages.

The Our Team and Player Development pages.
