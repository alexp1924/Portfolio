AI Chef App

A concept app that uses AI to help people decide what to cook, based on the ingredients they have, their diet and what they like.

Role UX / UI Designer Type Concept App Year 2026 Tools Figma, Miro
Problem

Most recipe apps start with a search box. I wanted to start with the ingredients people already have.

What I did

A short university project. I worked out where AI could actually help, then designed and prototyped the app in Figma.

Result

A concept covering onboarding, ingredient scanning, meal suggestions and nutrition.

Defining the experience

Where could AI help?

Instead of adding AI everywhere, I split the app into five areas and looked at where AI would actually be useful, from recognising ingredients to giving nutrition tips.

Onboarding preferences Meal recommendations Recipe discovery Ingredient scanning Nutrition insights
Decision 01 / 03

Start with your ingredients

The problem
Searching for recipes only works if you already know what you want to cook.
What I designed
You take a photo of your ingredients and the app suggests meals you can make with them. This also helps cut down on food waste.
Decision 02 / 03

Keep nutrition simple

The problem
Too much nutrition data can be overwhelming and feel a bit judgemental.
What I designed
The app shows your daily intake with just one tip from the AI, so it feels helpful rather than preachy.
Decision 03 / 03

Personal suggestions

The problem
Normal recipe apps don't know what you like or what you've already eaten.
What I designed
Suggestions are based on what you said you like when signing up, your past meals and your nutrition.
Final screens
Reflection

What I'd test next.

This was a short concept project, so I didn't do much research or testing. If I took it further, I'd test whether people trust the ingredient scanning, and whether the AI tips actually change what they cook.
