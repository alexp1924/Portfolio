Manchester United Graphics

Graphics I make for fun, inspired by Manchester United, from player stat graphics to matchday lineups.

Type Graphic Design Focus Sports Visuals Year 2026 Tools Photoshop, Illustrator
Overview

A personal project.

Alongside my UX work, I make graphics inspired by Manchester United. I've supported them for a long time, and I enjoy combining football with design, especially when it comes to showing player stats.

It's a chance to try out different styles, typography and layouts outside of product design.

Player graphics

Player stat graphics.

Graphics that show how a player did in a match. Each one pairs a photo of the player with their key stats from the game.

Match lineups

Matchday lineups.

Lineup graphics showing the starting eleven, designed to look good while still being easy to read.
