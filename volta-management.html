04 / 05 · Case study

Volta Management

Designing a professional digital presence for an elite football development agency — helping players progress from grassroots to professional level.

Role UX / UI Designer Type Client Project Year 2025 Tools Figma
Problem

Volta had no website, so parents and players had no way to see the principles, processes and models behind its player development.

What I did

Researched with a founder, the owners and players, then designed the Player Development and About Us pages in a team of three.

Result

Testers understood the site's purpose quickly, and the client praised the storytelling and player pathway.

Research

Starting from the people, because there was no website to audit.

Volta had never had a website, so there was nothing existing to review. The research came straight from the people involved.

A founder walked us through a presentation of everything that goes into their player development: the models they follow, and why they prioritise long-term development over short-term results. We also spoke to the owners and to players about what they would have wanted to see on a website.

Development follows defined modelsVolta works to several distinct models, which became the backbone of the Player Development page.
Long-term over short-termThe founder framed the offer around long-term player development, not quick results.
Parents and players are the audienceThe site needed to show families what Volta stands for and how a player develops with them.
My role

Three people, one site. I owned Player Development.

As part of a three-person team, I was responsible for the Player Development page, the About Us UX/UI design and the promotional video concept.

Decision 01 / 03

Make Volta's principles and models easy to follow

What I found
Volta's player development is built on clear principles, processes and models, but parents and players had nowhere to see them.
What I did
I structured the Player Development page around those models as cards, each with a short summary and a “Read more” option, so families can scan the whole approach quickly and open the detail on what matters to them.
Decision 02 / 03

Lead with a player's story, not a list of services

What I found
Volta values long-term development over short-term results, so the journey itself is the offer.
What I did
We proposed a homepage video following a player from grassroots to signing a professional contract, capturing both authenticity and aspiration.
Decision 03 / 03

Show reach at a glance

What I found
With no website before, visitors had no way to see how far Volta's network reached.
What I did
An interactive world map shows where Volta has placed players, giving the brand scale and credibility without relying on text.
Wireframes

From low-fidelity sketches to mid-fidelity layouts.

Wireframes set the structure and content hierarchy across key pages before any visual design.

Testing

People understood it quickly. One page was harder to find.

What we foundPeople quickly understood the purpose of the website and found the calls to action clear. The Player Development page took longer to locate than expected.
What we changedWe improved the navigation hierarchy and call-to-action placement, and kept strong hover interactions to help people discover pages.
Outcome

Strong client feedback.

The client, Beng'yela at Volta, praised the team's understanding of the market and the brand's developmental positioning, highlighting the storytelling homepage, the Global Talent Network map and the way the player pathway and services were presented.

Final design

The Our Team and Player Development pages.
